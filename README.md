# Hawaiʻi Postcards

**Play:** https://a-c-pack.github.io/hawaii-postcards/

**Hawaiʻi Postcards** is a short vocabulary game for students in Academic English 1 at BYU–Hawaii. Each
week, you visit a different place on Oʻahu with your roommate Tavita and practice 10 academic words. With
every correct answer, the scene comes a little more alive: the sun rises, the haze lifts, and Kevin the
turtle comes ashore. Each week takes about 15 minutes. You fill in trail signs, turn casual postcards
into academic writing, find word partners in the tide pools, and finish with a postcard you can download
for Canvas.

## How your words were chosen

Your words are chosen for your major. They come from the **New Academic Word List (NAWL)**, a list of the
words used most often in university reading and writing. Every word was rated for how useful it is in
each of 25 BYU–Hawaii majors. When you choose your major, the game puts the words most useful for your
field first. Among words that are equally useful, the most common ones come first. The top 140 words
become your list for Academic English 1, 10 words a week.

In the first weeks, most majors share many of the same words, like *obtain*, *aspect* and *impact*,
because every field needs them. After that, your list moves toward the words you'll see most in your own
classes.

## Saving your progress

Progress saves in your browser after every answer. To continue on another computer, click 💾 (top right,
any time while playing) to download a progress file, then use **Load progress** on the title screen of
the other computer.

## About this repo

This repo holds only the built game. It's generated from the source project by `publish.py`, so edit
there, not here.

Educational and non-commercial. The paintings were made with Ideogram (non-commercial weights); the
voices are designed from text descriptions, with no real person's voice cloned.
